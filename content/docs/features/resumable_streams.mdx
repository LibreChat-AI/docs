title: Resumable Streams
icon: Zap
description: A response keeps generating when you refresh, lose your connection, or switch tabs, and picks up where it is when you come back.
---

A response in LibreChat does not depend on your browser staying connected. Generation runs on the server, so if you refresh, lose the network, or close the tab, the model keeps going, and when you open the conversation again you see everything produced so far followed by the rest as it streams.

## What you will notice

- Refreshing the page mid-response no longer loses it. The text already streamed comes back at once and streaming continues.
- A dropped connection reconnects and fills in whatever arrived while you were offline, including tool calls and agent steps.
- You can start a long response, go to another conversation or close the tab, and come back to the finished answer.
- The same conversation open in two tabs, or on your laptop and your phone, shows the same response streaming in both.

## Limits

The response survives your connection, not the server. If the LibreChat process handling the generation stops, the generation stops with it.

On a deployment that runs several LibreChat instances, resuming works across instances only when the admin has enabled Redis for streams. Without it, each instance keeps its streams in its own memory, so a reconnect routed to a different instance cannot resume the response.

## Setup

No setup is needed for a single LibreChat instance: streams are kept in memory and resume out of the box. Deployments with more than one instance behind a load balancer need Redis, which streams use automatically once `USE_REDIS` is on; see [Redis: Resumable Streams](/docs/configuration/redis#resumable-streams).
