The sidebar holds your chat history and the other panels your deployment turns on, such as Bookmarks, Prompts, Memories and Skills. Collapse it with the sidebar toggle (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) when you want the whole screen for a conversation.

## Pin and organize chats

The **Pinned** section at the top mixes pinned chats with your favorite models, Agents and presets. With a mouse you can drag items to reorder them, drag a chat onto **Pinned** to pin it, onto a [Project](/docs/features/projects) to file it there, or onto **Chats** to take it out of its project. On touch screens, use the chat's menu instead.

Pinning, archiving and reordering do not count as activity, so they do not move a chat to the top of a list sorted by **Updated**.

## Filter and Sort the Chat List

The **Filter and sort chats** button next to the **Chats** heading opens a menu with these rows:

- **Show**: **Active chats** or **Archived chats**.
- **Sort**: **Updated**, **Created** or **Title** (plus **Date Archived** in the archived view), and under **Order**, newest or oldest first, or **A to Z** and **Z to A** for titles.
- **Filter**: narrow the list by **Updated** or **Created** date (**Today**, **Previous 7 days**, **Previous 30 days**, **Previous year**), by **Endpoint**, by **Has attachments**, by **Shared only** (chats with an active shared link), or by **Bookmarks**. Type in **Search filters** to find one quickly.
- **Show project chats**: chats filed in a project normally appear only under that project. Turn this on to list them under **Chats** too.

The button shows a count when the list differs from the default. **Reset** at the top of the menu puts everything back; **Clear filters** clears only the filters. Show and Filter choices last until you reload the page or sign out, while the sort order and **Show project chats** are remembered in this browser. Your administrator may limit how many endpoints one filter can include with [`conversationList`](/docs/configuration/librechat_yaml/object_structure/config#conversationlist).

## Unread Replies

When a reply finishes while you are in another chat or another tab, that chat gets an unread dot in the list. The dot clears once you open the chat and the reply is on screen. Read state is saved to your account, so opening the chat on your phone clears it on your laptop too.

To come back to a chat later, choose **Mark as unread** from its menu in the list. It is not offered for the chat you have open or one that is already unread. Temporary chats never show as unread, and neither does a reply stopped before it produced anything.

Tab counts, desktop notifications and sounds are set in [Settings](/docs/features/settings#notifications).

## Chat options

The **...** button in the chat header opens **Chat options** for the open chat: **Share**, **Export**, **Rename**, **Pin**, **Change project**, **Duplicate**, **Archive** and **Delete**. A dot on the button means the chat has an active shared link. **Pin**, **Change project** and **Archive** are not offered for a temporary chat, and the menu is hidden on a new chat. See [Shareable Links](/docs/features/shareable_links) and [exporting a conversation](/docs/features/import_convos#export-a-conversation).

Each chat in the sidebar has its own menu with **Pin**, **Mark as unread**, **Rename**, **Archive** and **Delete**. While you hold <kbd>Shift</kbd>, the open chat's row shows one-click archive and delete buttons.

## Mobile Drawer

On a phone, the sidebar is a full-screen drawer. Swipe right across the conversation to open it and swipe left inside it to close it; scrolling, typing and code blocks that scroll sideways keep their own gestures. The drawer header has the panel switcher, **New chat** and your account menu.

If you would rather keep the conversation in view, turn on **Show chat beside the sidebar on mobile** in **Settings > General**. The drawer then stops short of the edge, leaving a strip of the chat visible, and tapping the strip closes the drawer.

## Archived Conversations

Archiving hides a chat from your list without deleting it. Open **Archived chats** from the account menu, or switch **Show** to **Archived chats** in the [chat list menu](#filter-and-sort-the-chat-list), to find, restore or delete archived chats. They sort by when you archived them; chats archived before LibreChat recorded that date fall back to their creation date.

To clear out your whole list at once, use **Archive all chats** in **Settings > Data & Privacy**.
