The `registration` block controls which social login buttons appear and in what order, which email domains may sign up or sign in, and a few timing settings for social and OpenID logins. Turning registration and social login on or off is done with environment variables; see [Authentication](/docs/configuration/authentication).

## Example

```yaml filename="Registration Object Structure"
# Example Registration Object Structure
registration:
  socialLogins: ["google", "facebook", "github", "discord", "openid"]
  allowedDomains:
    - "gmail.com"
    - "protonmail.com"
  oauthStateTtlMs: 600000
  openidDiscovery:
    startupAttempts: 1
    retryDelayMs: 5000
```

## allowedDomains

**Key:**
<OptionTable
  options={[
    ['allowedDomains', 'Array of Strings', 'A list of email domains allowed to register and sign in.', 'Users whose email domain is not listed cannot register, and are also turned away when signing in through a social provider, OpenID, SAML, or LDAP, and when requesting a password reset. Domains must match exactly (subdomains are not included) and are compared case-insensitively. When omitted or empty, every domain is allowed.'],
  ]}
/>

**Example:**
```yaml filename="registration / allowedDomains"
allowedDomains:
  - "gmail.com"
  - "protonmail.com"
```

## oauthStateTtlMs

**Key:**
<OptionTable
  options={[
    ['oauthStateTtlMs', 'Number', 'How long, in milliseconds, a started social or OpenID login may take to reach its callback.', 'Between 60000 (1 minute) and 3600000 (1 hour). Default: 600000 (10 minutes).'],
  ]}
/>

**Example:**


## openidDiscovery

Controls how LibreChat retries fetching the OpenID provider's discovery document. Each unset field falls back to its environment variable (`OPENID_DISCOVERY_RETRY_ATTEMPTS`, `OPENID_DISCOVERY_RETRY_DELAY_MS`), then to the default.

**Keys:**
<OptionTable
  options={[
    ['startupAttempts', 'Number', 'Discovery attempts made before startup continues.', 'Between 0 and 100. 0 skips the startup attempts and only retries in the background. Default: 1.'],
    ['retryDelayMs', 'Number', 'Milliseconds between startup and background discovery attempts.', 'Between 100 and 3600000. Default: 5000.'],
  ]}
/>

**Example:**
```yaml filename="registration / openidDiscovery"
openidDiscovery:
  startupAttempts: 3
  retryDelayMs: 5000
```
